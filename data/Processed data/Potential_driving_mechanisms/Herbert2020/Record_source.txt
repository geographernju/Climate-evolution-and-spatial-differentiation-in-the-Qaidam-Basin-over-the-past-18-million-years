Herbert et al. (2020). Bihemispheric Warming in the Miocene Climatic Optimum as Seen From the Danish North Sea.
Journal: Paleoceanography and Paleoclimatology 35, e2020PA003935.
Publication: https://doi.org/10.1029/2020PA003935
PDF: https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10386318
Data: https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.921243
Bundle, including age-model constraints: https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.921249
Site: Sdr. Vium borehole, Danish North Sea.
Plot: published sand-corrected BACON age (ka)/1000 and Muller et al. (1998) SST column, restricted to 0-18 Ma.
Both SST records in the comparison panel use the published Muller calibration; no new proxy calibration was fitted.
Display: raw observations and line through median replicate values at identical ages; no smoothing or binning. Gaps greater than 1.5 Myr remain unconnected.
Missing values and flagged entries are omitted. The full original dataset, including ages older than 18 Ma and alternative age models and calibrations, is retained.
Interpretation: remote marine temperature comparison; age uncertainties and possible seasonality limit event-level alignment with terrestrial MAT.

Classification: Potential driving mechanisms; large-scale ocean thermal background. Included in Figure 2, not Figure 1.
